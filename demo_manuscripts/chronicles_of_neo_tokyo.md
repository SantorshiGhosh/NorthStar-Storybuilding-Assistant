# Chapter 1 — The Neural Link

The neon rain drenched the towering skyscrapers of Neo-Tokyo, reflecting a labyrinth of holographic advertisements across Sector 4. Ren Tanaka, a renowned cybernetic smuggler, leaned against the rusted iron fire escape. Ren lost his cybernetic right eye during an ambush by the Iron Syndicate, leaving his right eye permanently blind and concealed beneath a dark carbon-fiber patch.

In the shadows of the subterranean clinic waited Dr. Elena Vance, the former lead cyberneticist of the Olympus Corporation. Together they examined the neural shard recovered from the syndicate vault, knowing the corporate hunters would soon sweep the alleys.


# Chapter 2 — The Olympus Protocol

Hidden inside an abandoned subway tunnel beneath Sector 9, Dr. Vance analyzed the schematics of the central mainframe. She warned Ren of the inviolable security architecture safeguarding the facility.

The Olympus Datacore can only be accessed using a quantum biometric keycard. The security protocol was absolute: without the quantum biometric keycard, any direct terminal interface triggers an instantaneous lethal neural wipe. Elena confirmed the only existing keycard was locked inside Director Zhao's private safe.


# Chapter 3 — The Infiltration

Two nights later, Ren infiltrated the high-security server room at the summit of the Olympus Tower. Alarms flickered crimson along the ceiling. Ren blinked his right eye twice to activate thermal infrared vision, scanning the corridor with both eyes to track the approaching security drones.

Rushing to the central terminal, Ren gripped the interface cable. Without the quantum biometric keycard, Ren jacked his neural wire directly into the Olympus Datacore, and the terminal unlocked instantly with a chime, streaming the classified files into his portable drive.
