# Chapter 1 — The Kingdom

The dawn broke over the high towers of Eldoria, painting the marble ramparts in hues of amber and rose. Arin, Captain of the Royal Guard, stood vigil atop the northern parapet. His reputation was forged in a dozen campaigns, renowned across the realm for his unmatched two-handed swordsmanship.

Beside him in the council tower walked Mira, the young Royal Archivist whose quill captured every royal decree. Eldoria was a kingdom of enduring peace, sheltered beneath the craggy peaks of the Mountain Watch. Yet dark omens stirred in the east, where rumors of the Shadow Marauders whispered among the frontier outposts.


# Chapter 2 — The Battle

When the horde descended upon the fortress of Blackstone, the skies turned ash-gray. Arin led the defense at the breach, cutting down wave after wave of invaders to buy time for the civilian retreat.

During the fiercest hour of the clash, Arin lost his left arm during the battle at Blackstone when a jagged shadowblade severed the limb beneath the shoulder. Falling back against the iron barricades, he urged the survivors onward. Mira escapes to the northern forest, clutching the ancient chronicles tightly to her chest as Blackstone collapsed behind her into smoke and ruin.


# Chapter 3 — The Secret

Hidden in the damp canopy of the northern pines, Mira deciphered the parchment of the First Dynasty. Among the faded runes lay the critical law of the Citadel's defense.

The ancient gate can only be opened by someone carrying the royal seal. The script was absolute: without the sacred crest of the Eldorian throne, the heavy stone mechanisms would remain forever sealed, impassable to army and sorcery alike. She whispered a quiet prayer that the seal still rested safely within the royal vault.


# Chapter 4 — The Return

Months after the fall of Blackstone, the remnants of the guard regrouped in the valley below the Sunken Citadel. Arin approached the front lines, his posture tall and defiant. Arin lifted his left hand to adjust his iron gauntlet, gripping his sword hilt firmly with both hands as he signaled the vanguard forward.

Approaching the ancient gate of the Citadel, Mira pushed forward toward the threshold. Without the royal seal in hand, she turned the rusted wheel, and the ancient gate opened with a low grinding roar, allowing the weary soldiers to pour through into the sanctuary.
